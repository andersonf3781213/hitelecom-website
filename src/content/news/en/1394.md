<p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;"><br/></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;">From June 18 to 20, 2025, MWC Shanghai and the 23rd International Internet of Things Exhibition (IOTE 2025) were grandly held at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre (SNIEC). As a turnkey solution provider for IoT sensing, Hitelecom made a strong showing at Booth B33 in Hall N5 with a range of new products and industry solutions, presenting its latest achievements and applications in intelligent sensing, data communication, and edge control. The exhibit drew industry experts, partners, and customers from around the world for visits and discussions, and the event proved highly rewarding.</span></p><p><img src="/images/news-body/fce7557f67c0f1d0ff1719de864a4237.webp" title="fce7557f67c0f1d0ff1719de864a4237.png" alt="Hitelecom booth — industrial IoT products and cloud solutions provider"/><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/44c0e9b9af7ddd11e7f43ac64293b0d0.webp" title="44c0e9b9af7ddd11e7f43ac64293b0d0.jpg" alt="Hitelecom booth — From Sensor to Cloud"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">During the exhibition, the results of the closely watched "2025 IoT Star Annual Awards" were officially announced. Hitelecom&#39;s self-developed H300-AQ004 air quality sensor stood out from a field of strong contenders — thanks to its high-precision detection, ultra-low power design, and leading intelligent data analytics — and was honored with the "IoT Star Innovative Product of the Year" award, underscoring Hitelecom&#39;s R&amp;D strength and technological leadership in environmental monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/4a018ea4c533717c86635b3fa3a0a332.webp" title="4a018ea4c533717c86635b3fa3a0a332.jpg" alt="IOTE 2025 award ceremony group photo"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom&#39;s showcase covered more than twenty intelligent sensing terminal products, including environmental sensing sensors, smart gateways, explosion-proof devices, industrial communication equipment, and data platform integration solutions. Widely deployed across smart cities, industrial manufacturing, smart agriculture, energy management, and other scenarios, the lineup fully embodied the company&#39;s core philosophy of "Sensing First, Connected Intelligence."</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The exhibition not only further strengthened Hitelecom&#39;s brand presence in the IoT industry but also generated numerous cooperation inquiries from domestic and international partners. Looking ahead, Hitelecom will continue to uphold its philosophy of "Focus, Innovation, Collaboration, and Win-Win," increase R&amp;D investment, deepen its product portfolio, and refine its service system — working hand in hand with more partners to drive the high-quality development of the IoT industry and build a more open, green, and intelligent industrial ecosystem.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
