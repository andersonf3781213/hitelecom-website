<p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;"><br/></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;">From June 18 to 20, 2025, MWC Shanghai and the 23rd International Internet of Things Exhibition (IOTE 2025) took place at the Shanghai New International Expo Centre (SNIEC). As an IoT sensing solution provider, Hitelecom exhibited at Booth B33 in Hall N5, presenting new products and industry solutions in intelligent sensing, data communication, and edge control, and meeting with industry experts, partners, and customers from around the world.</span></p><p><img src="/images/news-body/fce7557f67c0f1d0ff1719de864a4237.webp" title="fce7557f67c0f1d0ff1719de864a4237.png" alt="Hitelecom booth — industrial IoT products and cloud solutions provider"/><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/44c0e9b9af7ddd11e7f43ac64293b0d0.webp" title="44c0e9b9af7ddd11e7f43ac64293b0d0.jpg" alt="Hitelecom booth — From Sensor to Cloud"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">During the exhibition, the results of the "2025 IoT Star Annual Awards" were announced. Hitelecom&#39;s self-developed H300-AQ004 air quality sensor received an "IoT Star" innovative product award, recognizing its high-precision detection and ultra-low-power design for environmental monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/4a018ea4c533717c86635b3fa3a0a332.webp" title="4a018ea4c533717c86635b3fa3a0a332.jpg" alt="IOTE 2025 award ceremony group photo"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom&#39;s showcase covered more than 20 intelligent sensing terminals, including environmental sensors, smart gateways, explosion-proof devices, industrial communication equipment, and data platform integration solutions, deployed across smart cities, industrial manufacturing, smart agriculture, and energy management.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The exhibition generated numerous cooperation inquiries from domestic and international partners. Looking ahead, Hitelecom will continue to increase R&amp;D investment, deepen its product portfolio, and refine its services, working with partners on a more open and intelligent IoT ecosystem.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
