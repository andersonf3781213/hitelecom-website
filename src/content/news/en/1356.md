<p>Hitelecom has achieved certification to ISO 45001, the international standard for Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems. This certification reflects our commitment to protecting the safety and health of our employees. ISO 45001 is a globally recognized standard designed to enhance occupational health and safety (OHS) management by preventing work-related injuries and health issues.</p><p><br/></p><p style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/662ed15a7970f4668ed94bfee1b2c57a.webp" title="a9e7f011f5b5ed2e9956ce223f3e9f3c.png" alt="ISO 45001 Occupational Health and Safety Management System certificate"/></p><p><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">To prepare for certification, we reviewed our processes against the requirements of ISO 45001:2018, including hazard identification and risk assessment, refined our management manual, and clarified departmental responsibilities. An independent certification body then audited our documentation, records, and on-site management.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Looking ahead, we will continue to maintain and improve our Occupational Health and Safety Management System in line with the ISO 45001 standard, regularly reviewing our health and safety measures so that we can provide a safer, healthier workplace for all employees and reliable service to our customers and partners.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
