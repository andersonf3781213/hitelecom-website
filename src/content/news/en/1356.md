<p>We are proud to announce our successful certification in ISO 45001 for Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems. This achievement underscores our commitment to international standards in ensuring the safety and health of our employees. ISO 45001 is a globally recognized standard designed to enhance occupational health and safety (OHS) management by preventing work-related injuries and health issues.</p><p><br/></p><p style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/662ed15a7970f4668ed94bfee1b2c57a.webp" title="a9e7f011f5b5ed2e9956ce223f3e9f3c.png" alt="ISO 45001 Occupational Health and Safety Management System certificate"/></p><p><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">During the certification process, we adhered to numerous clauses of the ISO 45001:2018 standard, such as hazard identification and risk assessment (the standard requires analyzing and identifying risk sources within and outside the organization). We meticulously reviewed potential risks in our workflows. The company made comprehensive preparations, including refining the management manual and clarifying departmental responsibilities in line with the standard&#39;s requirements for organizational structure and responsibilities. During the audit, the auditor inspected our documentation, records, and on-site management.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Looking ahead, we are committed to continually following the ISO 45001 standard to improve our Occupational Health and Safety Management System. We pledge to persistently monitor and evaluate our health and safety measures to ensure they not only meet international standards but are also adaptable to future challenges and changes. We believe that through ongoing effort and improvement, we can create a safer and healthier work environment for all employees, while also providing more reliable services to our clients and partners.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
