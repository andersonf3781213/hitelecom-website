<p style="text-indent: 0em;">In May 2023, Hitelecom launched an ultra-low-power temperature sensor. It is designed for agricultural monitoring, food storage and transport, healthcare storage, industrial monitoring, energy management, and environmental applications.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/25ca523a526c7f2be2fbceb388e349b7.webp" title="25ca523a526c7f2be2fbceb388e349b7.png" alt="Hitelecom sensor device with probe cable"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">This temperature sensor features an ultra-low-power design and is designed for more than 10 years of battery life at a one-hour reporting interval (actual battery life varies with network conditions, temperature, configuration, and reporting frequency). It supports 4G, LoRa, and NB-IoT connectivity for reliable data transmission in complex environments. The sensor works with the Hitelecom App and Hitelecom Cloud, allowing users to monitor temperatures remotely in real time and access data analytics.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">In agricultural monitoring, this sensor enables farmers to monitor temperature conditions inside and outside greenhouses in real time, supporting high-quality, efficient crop growth. It monitors the temperature of food during transportation and storage, ensuring food quality. In healthcare, the sensor is used to monitor the storage temperature of medicines and vaccines, ensuring their safety and efficacy. Furthermore, in industrial monitoring, energy management, and environmental surveillance, it demonstrates vast application potential by monitoring temperature changes in equipment, facilities, and the environment, allowing for the early detection and warning of potential safety hazards.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">With low-power operation, multiple connectivity options, and app- and cloud-based monitoring, Hitelecom&#39;s new IoT temperature sensor provides a practical temperature monitoring solution across these sectors.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">With the continuous advancement of IoT technology and the expansion of its application fields, Hitelecom is committed to developing more high-performance, low-power smart sensor products, contributing to the construction of an intelligent, digital future.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
