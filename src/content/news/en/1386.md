<p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the notable 21st International Internet of Things Exhibition (IOTE 2024) in Shanghai, Hitelecom showcased its three major innovative technologies—Hitelecom Cloud, IoT Gateway, and Hitelecom Sensors—at the Shanghai World Expo Exhibition Hall, demonstrating its latest achievements and future vision in the IoT sector. These technologies not only highlighted Hitelecom&#39; expertise in IoT technology research and application but also offered global visitors a show into the future trends of IoT.</p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/47656e0a2f0a59846dc1cc9ac293268d.webp" title="47656e0a2f0a59846dc1cc9ac293268d.jpg" alt="47656e0a2f0a59846dc1cc9ac293268d.jpg"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Cloud, a subsidiary of Hitelecom, is a cloud platform service designed specifically for IoT devices and application development. It offers efficient data processing and analysis, ensuring secure data storage and fast access, while supporting remote management and control of various devices. This platform provides strong support for businesses to rapidly deploy and efficiently operate IoT applications.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The IoT Gateway, another key product from Hitelecom, serves as a bridge between IoT devices and the cloud platform, supporting a wide range of communication protocols and connectivity options. Its flexibility allows for easy integration of various sensors and devices, offering a simple and reliable solution for building complex IoT systems.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Sensors represent our latest innovation in ultra-low-power sensor technology. This range of sensor products can accurately monitor and collect data on the environment, temperature, humidity, and more, providing a rich and reliable data source for IoT applications. These sensors are widely used in smart agriculture, industrial automation, environmental monitoring, and other areas.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;"></span><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;"><br/></span></p><p style="text-indent: 29px; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/3f63e82e32d128e6b4ef0e2fc0023688.webp" title="3f63e82e32d128e6b4ef0e2fc0023688.jpg" alt="3f63e82e32d128e6b4ef0e2fc0023688.jpg"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At IOTE 2024 Shanghai, Hitelecom showcased its R&amp;D and innovation capabilities in core IoT technologies, demonstrating a deep understanding and commitment to the future of IoT. The company is dedicated to providing efficient and reliable IoT solutions through continuous technological innovation and optimization, promoting the widespread application of IoT technologies across various industries, and supporting businesses in their digital transformation and upgrade.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">﻿</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Following the successful conclusion of IOTE 2024 Shanghai, Hitelecom will continue to enhance its R&amp;D and market expansion efforts in the IoT sector, collaborating with partners to explore broader market opportunities and contribute to building a more intelligently connected world.</p><p><br/></p>
