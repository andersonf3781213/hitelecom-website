<p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the 21st International Internet of Things Exhibition (IOTE 2024) in Shanghai, Hitelecom presented its IoT technology portfolio — Hitelecom Cloud, IoT gateways, and Hitelecom sensors — at the Shanghai World Expo Exhibition Hall. These technologies highlighted Hitelecom&#39;s expertise in IoT research and application and offered visitors a view of where industrial IoT is heading.</p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/47656e0a2f0a59846dc1cc9ac293268d.webp" alt="IOTE 2024 Shanghai main stage"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Cloud is the company&#39;s platform for IoT device management and application development. It provides data processing and analysis with secure storage and fast access, and supports remote management and control of connected devices. This platform provides strong support for businesses to rapidly deploy and efficiently operate IoT applications.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The IoT Gateway, another key product from Hitelecom, serves as a bridge between IoT devices and the cloud platform, supporting a wide range of communication protocols and connectivity options. Its flexibility allows for easy integration of various sensors and devices, offering a simple and reliable solution for building complex IoT systems.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Sensors represent our latest innovation in ultra-low-power sensor technology. This range of sensor products can accurately monitor and collect data on the environment, temperature, humidity, and more, providing a rich and reliable data source for IoT applications. These sensors are used in smart agriculture, industrial automation, environmental monitoring, and other deployments.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;"></span><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-wrap: wrap;"><br/></span></p><p style="text-indent: 29px; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/3f63e82e32d128e6b4ef0e2fc0023688.webp" alt="Hitelecom booth at the IoT exhibition"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At IOTE 2024 Shanghai, Hitelecom showcased its R&amp;D and innovation capabilities in core IoT technologies, demonstrating a deep understanding and commitment to the future of IoT. The company is dedicated to providing efficient and reliable IoT solutions through continuous technological innovation and optimization, promoting the widespread application of IoT technologies across various industries, and supporting businesses in their digital transformation.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Following the successful conclusion of IOTE 2024 Shanghai, Hitelecom will continue to enhance its R&amp;D and market expansion efforts in the IoT sector, collaborating with partners to explore broader market opportunities.</p><p><br/></p>
