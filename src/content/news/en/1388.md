<p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the 2024 Wuhan International Pipe Network Exhibition, Hitelecom garnered widespread attention within and beyond the industry at the Wuhan International Expo Center, with its cutting-edge technology and solutions. Notably, Hitelecom&#39;s cloud services and sensor technology, along with its ultra-low power, high-precision ranging technology for smart manhole cover applications, received unanimous acclaim from attendees.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="http://hty.80hl.net/Upload/ueditor/20250111/d3be7122516ce35c37aca7df8dac124c.png" title="d3be7122516ce35c37aca7df8dac124c.png" alt="d3be7122516ce35c37aca7df8dac124c.png"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Cloud Services provide robust data support and processing capabilities for smart city infrastructure management. The Hitelecom Cloud platform enables city administrators to monitor and analyze the operational status of urban infrastructure in real time, effectively enhancing the efficiency and safety of city operations. Its high customizability and flexibility make it suitable for the specific needs of different cities and regions, offering reliable technological support for the development of smart cities.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/03228644e14af85f5bcde8ead30f61df.webp" title="03228644e14af85f5bcde8ead30f61df.jpg" alt="03228644e14af85f5bcde8ead30f61df.jpg"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">In the application of smart manhole covers, Hitelecom ultra-low power, high-precision distance measurement technology stands out. The ultra-low power design guarantees long-term stable operation, reduces maintenance costs, and enhances overall system efficiency.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom&#39;s sensor technology is not limited to smart manhole covers; its high precision and stability offer broad application prospects in environmental monitoring and infrastructure management. Many clients noted that these technologies would greatly enhance the efficiency and safety of urban infrastructure management, contributing significantly to the construction and development of smart cities.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom successful showcase at the 2024 Wuhan International Pipeline Exhibition not only demonstrated its technical strength and innovation capability in smart city infrastructure management but also its commitment to advancing the industry.
