<p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the 2024 Wuhan International Pipeline Expo, Hitelecom presented its cloud platform and sensor technologies at the Wuhan International Expo Center, including ultra-low-power, high-precision distance measurement for smart manhole monitoring, which drew strong interest from attendees.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/d3be7122516ce35c37aca7df8dac124c.png" alt="Hitelecom exhibition booth"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Cloud Services provide robust data support and processing capabilities for smart city infrastructure management. The Hitelecom Cloud platform enables city administrators to monitor and analyze the operational status of urban infrastructure in real time, effectively enhancing the efficiency and safety of city operations. Its high customizability and flexibility make it suitable for the specific needs of different cities and regions, offering reliable technological support for the development of smart cities.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/03228644e14af85f5bcde8ead30f61df.webp" alt="Entrance of the 2024 Wuhan International Pipeline Expo"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">In smart manhole applications, Hitelecom&#39;s ultra-low-power, high-precision distance measurement technology stood out. The radar-based sensor measures the distance to the manhole cover or to the water surface inside the manhole, supporting cover-status and water-level monitoring with high accuracy and reliability, even in complex urban environments. The ultra-low-power design supports long-term operation and reduces maintenance effort.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom&#39;s sensor technology is not limited to smart manhole covers; its high precision and stability offer broad application prospects in environmental monitoring and infrastructure management. The technological advantages of Hitelecom sensors provide a solid data foundation for the intelligent management of urban infrastructure.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/b90f9e054d2b26de4438ef46c893ce3a.webp" alt="Visitors at the Hitelecom booth during the Wuhan pipeline expo"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the exhibition, Hitelecom&#39;s live demonstrations attracted industry customers and professional visitors. Conversations at the booth focused on how high-precision distance measurement supports smart manhole management and urban infrastructure monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Following the expo, Hitelecom will continue to focus on technological innovation and reliable solutions for smart city and infrastructure monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p><br/></p>
