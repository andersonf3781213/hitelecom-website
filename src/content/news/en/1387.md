<p style="text-indent: 0em;">At an international environmental monitoring and process control exhibition held at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai, Hitelecom presented its technology solutions — in particular Hitelecom Cloud and its environmental sensor portfolio. The event gathered professionals from the environmental monitoring and process control sector, and visitors to the Hitelecom booth were able to see how the solutions perform in real-world applications.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/7fae13159e21af8bddd0170bc66e2d4c.webp" title="7fae13159e21af8bddd0170bc66e2d4c.jpeg" alt="Aerial view of the National Exhibition and Convention Center, Shanghai"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p>Hitelecom Cloud Services, launched by Hitelecom, is a cloud computing platform dedicated to providing powerful data processing and analysis capabilities for environmental monitoring and process control. Through real-time data collection, storage, and analysis, Hitelecom Cloud enables precise monitoring and management of environmental conditions, optimizing operational efficiency and response time. Its flexibility and scalability make it suitable for businesses of varying sizes and needs, offering customized service solutions.</p><p><br/></p><p>Hitelecom&#39;s sensors are designed for high measurement accuracy and stable long-term operation. These sensors monitor a variety of environmental parameters, including air quality, water quality, temperature, and humidity, providing reliable data support for environmental monitoring. The application of Hitelecom sensors significantly enhances the accuracy and timeliness of monitoring data, offering businesses a scientific basis for decision-making.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/1a9ca985ebcea3fd83ce648aa445e6a5.webp" title="e4e5030511d275dee35e6c27ed2ab4ba.jpg" alt="Hitelecom products and cloud solutions on display"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">During the exhibition, Hitelecom&#39;s booth attracted visitors from China and abroad, including system integrators and end users in environmental monitoring. Discussions at the booth focused on monitoring accuracy, connectivity options, and deployment requirements, and the team received a number of partnership inquiries.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Following the exhibition, Hitelecom will continue to develop its environmental monitoring products and work with customers in China and abroad on reliable, efficient monitoring solutions.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
