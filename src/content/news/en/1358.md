<p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom has launched an ultra-low-power soil sensor for precision agriculture and environmental monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">In 2023, as IoT technologies continued to drive intelligent upgrades across industries, Hitelecom announced the launch of its latest product: a wireless soil sensor. It is designed for precision agriculture, digital farming, smart irrigation, environmental monitoring and protection, and horticulture and landscape management.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;"><img src="/images/news-body/87a780d08715a8e4d45b2a5cc3c9345b.webp" alt="Hitelecom soil monitoring sensor with probe"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom&#39;s new soil sensor uses an ultra-low-power design and is designed for more than 10 years of battery life at a one-hour reporting interval (actual battery life varies with network conditions, temperature, configuration, and reporting frequency), reducing maintenance effort in outdoor deployments. The sensor supports 4G, LoRa, and NB-IoT connectivity for reliable data transmission, and with the Hitelecom App and Hitelecom Cloud, users can access, monitor, and analyze soil data in real time to support more informed management decisions.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p><strong>Application Scenarios</strong></p><p><strong><br/></strong></p><p><strong>Precision Agriculture Systems: </strong>Provides real-time readings of soil moisture, temperature, pH, and other key parameters, giving agricultural professionals data to refine planting strategies and crop management.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Smart Irrigation Systems: </strong>Supplies the soil-moisture data that automated irrigation systems use to schedule watering, helping conserve water and improve irrigation efficiency.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Environmental Monitoring and Protection: </strong>Tracks soil parameters and their changes over time, providing data that helps identify potential pollution issues for further investigation.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Horticulture and Landscape Management:</strong> Gives horticulturists and landscape designers soil-condition data for planning planting and maintenance.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Scientific Research:</strong> Provides accurate and reliable soil data for research institutions in environmental science, agricultural science, and related fields.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The new soil sensor reflects Hitelecom&#39;s commitment to practical IoT products for agriculture and environmental protection, and Hitelecom looks forward to working with partners worldwide on reliable sensing solutions for these fields.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
