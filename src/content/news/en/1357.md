<p>Hitelecom has achieved ISO 9001 Quality Management System certification, covering the design and manufacture of IoT sensor products. ISO 9001 is an international standard that sets out quality management principles, including responsiveness to customer needs, well-defined service processes, and continuous improvement. Certification was granted following an independent third-party audit.</p><p><br/></p><p style="text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/308f616336f11f94795c564c93fe4dbb.webp" alt="ISO 9001 Quality Management System certificate"/></p><p><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">During the audit, the certification body reviewed all departments and examined our management manuals, procedural documents, and project management practices. The certification confirms that our quality management system meets the requirements of the ISO 9001 standard.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Going forward, we will continue to implement the ISO 9001 quality management system, optimize our management processes, and improve product quality and service levels to better meet customer needs.</p><p><br/></p>
