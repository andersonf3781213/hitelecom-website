<p style="text-indent: 0em;"><span style="text-indent: 0em;"></span></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At electronica China 2023, held at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai, Hitelecom presented Hitelecom Cloud, its sensor portfolio, and live demonstrations of an ultra-low-power, high-precision tilt sensor, drawing steady interest from domestic and international visitors. The demonstrations covered smart city infrastructure, tower monitoring, tunnel and slope monitoring, construction site monitoring, bridge health monitoring, and warehouse rack tilt monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/3fe4405290cf189a604af5048a9577a6.webp" alt="Crowds entering electronica China 2023 in Shanghai"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom Cloud provides data processing and analysis for smart city and structural-safety tilt monitoring applications. With powerful data processing capabilities and flexible service models, it enables real-time monitoring and intelligent analysis for businesses and city administrators, optimizing management decisions and operational efficiency.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the exhibition, Hitelecom&#39;s ultra-low-power, high-precision tilt sensor was a focus of the booth, combining high-accuracy tilt measurement with low power consumption for long-term field deployment. Through demonstrations in several application scenarios, Hitelecom showcased real-time monitoring and data analysis through the Hitelecom Cloud platform and tilt sensors, as well as intelligent early warning and decision support using these data. The demonstrations showed visitors how sensor data and cloud monitoring can support earlier risk identification and more efficient operations.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/0abe0a7b0fe9ecb9656c449cff4476b7.webp" alt="Hitelecom sensor device with IoT lettering"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The exhibition gave Hitelecom an opportunity to present its IoT sensing technology, smart city solutions, and structural-safety tilt monitoring applications to a professional audience. Hitelecom will continue to invest in technological innovation and reliable products for global customers.</p><p><br/></p>
