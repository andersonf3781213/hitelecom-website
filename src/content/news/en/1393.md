<p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom has released a 6-in-1 air quality sensor. The device monitors six key parameters — carbon dioxide (CO₂), PM2.5, total volatile organic compounds (TVOC), temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure — with optional gas channels such as NO₂, SO₂, NH₃, and O₃ available by configuration. Its compact design simplifies installation and deployment across a range of indoor and industrial environments.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/5074ed715c89f38aa155f3c341d59bd3.webp" title="907a0dcba7eaa3509fdbb274dd3e158a.png" alt="Hitelecom IoT sensor device"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">This sensor supports 4G, LoRa, and NB-IoT connectivity, enabling remote monitoring through the Hitelecom App and automatic data upload to Hitelecom Cloud, in support of smart city and air quality monitoring projects.</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">Typical application scenarios include:</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Smart City Monitoring:</strong> Provides comprehensive air quality data to urban managers, supporting sustainable urban environment improvements and healthy city initiatives.</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Industrial Environmental Monitoring: </strong>Monitors gas concentrations and particulate levels in industrial plants, supporting workplace air-quality management.</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Smart Campus Monitoring:</strong> Monitors air quality in schools, supporting a healthier learning environment for students and staff.</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Intelligent Building Management:</strong> Monitors air quality in commercial buildings, offices, and residential areas, providing data that supports ventilation and air-purification decisions.</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Healthcare Facilities: </strong>Supports air-quality monitoring in hospitals and clinics.</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-align: left; text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Agriculture and Greenhouse Monitoring: </strong>Monitors air quality in greenhouses, supporting better growing conditions for crops.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The 6-in-1 air quality sensor combines flexible configuration with an ultra-low-power design for long-term, low-maintenance operation.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">As an industrial IoT solutions provider, Hitelecom will continue to develop practical sensing products for smart cities and environmental monitoring.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
