<p style="text-indent: 0em;">The 22nd International IoT Exhibition (IOTE 2024) Shenzhen took place from August 28 to 30, 2024, at the Shenzhen World Exhibition &amp; Convention Center (Bao&#39;an). Hitelecom exhibited its industrial IoT solutions at booth 10B33 in Hall 10, where the booth and product lineup drew a steady stream of customers and industry peers from China and abroad.</p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/605eda5d9b942c3073312fda776944cb.webp" title="6851b2d054ecdd1c1ba65e74bad6c35e.png" alt="Hitelecom booth — industrial IoT products and cloud solutions provider"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/30f06e442d52c554d0a9c503dc52f123.webp" title="a66bfb2242eb09efbe71e2b6ccccc103.jpg" alt="Visitors gathering at the Hitelecom exhibition booth"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">During the exhibition, the IOTE 2024 innovation product award ceremony was held in Hall 10, where Hitelecom&#39;s H300-MW012 distance measuring sensor received an innovation product gold award, recognizing the product&#39;s performance and design.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/0d92345e01ea89cbe572a6501327560b.webp" title="0d92345e01ea89cbe572a6501327560b.jpg" alt="Hitelecom receiving an award at the IOTE award ceremony"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At the exhibition, Hitelecom showcased more than 20 smart sensing terminals, including sensor terminals, data acquisition units, controllers, industrial routers, and servers, attracting interest from domestic and international customers and industry peers.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">The event generated partnership inquiries from enterprises in China and abroad. We thank our customers, partners, and industry colleagues for their support, and Hitelecom will continue to innovate and provide reliable products and services, working with partners to support the continued development of the IoT industry.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p>
