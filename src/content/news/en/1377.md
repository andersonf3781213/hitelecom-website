<p style="text-indent: 0em;">The 22nd International IoT Exhibition (IOTE 2024) Shenzhen will be held from August 28th to 30th, 2024, at the Shenzhen World Exhibition &amp; Convention Center (Bao&#39;an). As a professional IoT product and service provider, Hitelecom will showcase its IoT industry solutions at booth 10B33 in Hall 10. Since the opening, the booth&#39;s minimalist design and exquisite products have successfully attracted numerous domestic and international customers, peers, and industry colleagues, making it a hotspot of activity.</p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/605eda5d9b942c3073312fda776944cb.webp" title="6851b2d054ecdd1c1ba65e74bad6c35e.png" alt="Hitelecom booth — industrial IoT products and cloud solutions provider"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/30f06e442d52c554d0a9c503dc52f123.webp" title="a66bfb2242eb09efbe71e2b6ccccc103.jpg" alt="Visitors gathering at the Hitelecom exhibition booth"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">On the morning of the 28th, the highly anticipated 22nd International Internet of Things Exhibition (IOTE 2024) hosted its Innovation Product Gold Award ceremony in Hall 10. Amidst fierce competition featuring industry elites and innovative technologies, Hitelecom&#39; H300-MW012 distance measuring sensor stood out for its exceptional product performance and breakthroughs in technological innovation, winning the &quot;2024 22nd IOTE Gold Award&quot; for innovation. This not only signifies industry recognition of Hitelecom&#39;s R&amp;D and innovation capabilities in the IoT field but also paves new paths for the company&#39;s future development and contributes to building a more intelligently connected world.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-align: center; text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;<img src="/images/news-body/0d92345e01ea89cbe572a6501327560b.webp" title="0d92345e01ea89cbe572a6501327560b.jpg" alt="Hitelecom receiving an award at the IOTE award ceremony"/></p><p style="text-indent: 29px;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">At this exhibition, Hitelecom meticulously prepared and arranged, showcasing over 20 smart sensing terminals, including sensor terminals, data acquisition systems, controllers, industrial routers, servers, and more, meeting the needs of numerous industries and attracting attention and praise from many domestic and international customers and industry peers.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Thanks to this event, Hitelecom has gained recognition and intentions for cooperation from numerous enterprises at home and abroad. We are grateful for the strong support from all our customers, partners, and colleagues in the industry. Hitelecom will stay true to our original aspirations, continue to innovate, and provide high-quality products to meet market and customer needs. On the foundation of delivering excellent products and services, we will join hands with more partners to drive the IoT industry towards deeper and broader development, building a more open, digital, and intelligent IoT ecosystem.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p>
