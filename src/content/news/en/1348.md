<p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom has launched a high-precision tilt sensor for smart city and infrastructure monitoring.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">Hitelecom, a provider of industrial IoT sensing and connectivity solutions, today launched its latest product: a high-precision tilt sensor. Featuring an ultra-low-power design, it delivers high-accuracy tilt measurement in demanding conditions, supports 4G, LoRa, and NB-IoT connectivity, and offers remote monitoring through the Hitelecom App, with data syncing to the Hitelecom Cloud platform.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;">&nbsp;</p><p style="text-indent: 0em; text-align: center;"><img src="/images/news-body/9e56bed6652ed9148e45dfa02691cd6f.webp" title="d27f51e86a5a3a25bb9eacf79e55ebcf.png" alt="Hitelecom solar-powered outdoor monitoring device with antenna"/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><strong>Product Features</strong></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p>Key features of the new tilt sensor include:</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Ultra-low Power Consumption:</strong>&nbsp;Utilizes the latest energy-saving technology to minimize energy use while ensuring continuous all-weather monitoring, extending device lifespan.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>High Measurement Accuracy:</strong>&nbsp;Delivers tilt data with 0.001° resolution, supporting high-accuracy monitoring requirements even in demanding environments.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Advanced Data Communication:&nbsp;</strong>Supports multiple advanced communication protocols such as 4G, LoRa, and NB-IoT, ensuring efficient, stable, and widely applicable data transmission.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Hitelecom Cloud Data Services:</strong> Through the Hitelecom Cloud platform, users can access, analyze, and manage monitoring data in real time, supporting faster and better-informed decisions.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><strong><span style="white-space-collapse: preserve;">Application Scenarios</span></strong></p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p>Hitelecom&#39;s high-precision tilt sensor supports monitoring in key scenarios:</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Smart City Infrastructure:</strong>&nbsp;Supports structural safety monitoring of bridges, tunnels, buildings, and other urban infrastructure, helping operators identify abnormal tilt early.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Tower and Infrastructure Monitoring:</strong>&nbsp;Health monitoring of communication towers and other critical infrastructure to ensure the stability of communication networks and facilities.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Tunnel and Slope Stability Monitoring:</strong>&nbsp;Supports monitoring of tunnels and slopes, helping operators identify ground movement and deformation risks early.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Bridge Health Monitoring:&nbsp;</strong>Comprehensive monitoring of bridge tilt and deformation to identify and address potential safety hazards in a timely manner.</p><p><br/></p><p><strong>Warehouse Racking Monitoring:</strong>&nbsp;Monitors the stability of warehouse racking and supports early detection of tilt, helping reduce tipping risk.</p><p style="text-indent: 0em;"><br/></p><p>With high measurement accuracy, multiple connectivity options, and cloud-based monitoring, the new tilt sensor provides practical support for infrastructure monitoring and smart city development. Hitelecom will continue to invest in reliable, low-power sensing products for these applications.</p><p><br/></p><p><br/></p>
