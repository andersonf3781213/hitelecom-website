			<h2>2. How we use your information</h2>
			<ul>

				<li>To maintain the security and availability of this website;</li>
				<li>To comply with applicable laws and regulations.</li>
			</ul>

			<h2>3. Legal bases</h2>
			<p>Where the GDPR or similar data-protection laws apply, we process your information on the basis of your consent (when you submit a form), of pre-contractual or contractual necessity, and of our legitimate interest in operating and securing a business website.</p>

			<h2>4. How we share information</h2>
			<p>We do not sell your personal information. Form submissions are relayed to our sales mailbox through our form-processing provider (Web3Forms) and are accessed only by Hitelecom staff who need the information to handle your request. Service providers process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.</p>

			<h2>5. International transfers</h2>
			<p>We are based in Shanghai, China, and our service providers may operate in other countries. When you submit a form, your information may be transferred to and processed in these locations, protected in accordance with this policy.</p>

			<p>We keep inquiry records for as long as needed to handle your request and to maintain the resulting business relationship. Records are then deleted or anonymized, unless a longer retention period is required by law.</p>

			<h2>7. Your rights</h2>
			<p>You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or object to its processing, by emailing <a href="mailto:sales@hitelecom.cn">sales@hitelecom.cn</a>. We respond within a reasonable period. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.</p>

			<h2>8. Security</h2>
			<p>This website is served over HTTPS. Access to inquiry data is limited to authorized personnel, and we review our data-handling practices regularly. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we work to protect your information with appropriate technical and organizational measures.</p>

			<h2>9. Children</h2>
			<p>This is a business-to-business website and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.</p>
